## Article 5 Your Data and Privacy

1. Games, instances, saves, settings and logs are stored on your own disk, with runtime data normally in `MoliFox/` beside the program. You manage these local materials; the project does not claim that your account information or game saves belong to the Developer.
2. Account sign-in tokens are stored only on this machine: on Windows they are encrypted with the system DPAPI (current user) and kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MoliFox\credentials`; **on Linux they are not encrypted** and are kept in `$XDG_DATA_HOME/molifox/credentials` (default `~/.local/share/molifox/credentials`). Do not share that directory, or your whole data directory, with anyone.
3. The Software does not send telemetry or usage statistics to the Developer and does not automatically upload crash reports. Playtime and recent launches may be recorded locally to display instance information. Diagnostic bundles are created only when you export them; logs are redacted where possible, but check their contents before sharing.
4. Online features send the information needed for the requested operation to the relevant service, such as your IP address, request contents, game and loader versions, and project IDs. Resource identification and update checks may also send file hashes or similar identifiers. These lookups do not thereby upload complete mod files or saves. Each third-party service has its own privacy policy.
